We answer water damage and mold calls in Chatsworth, CA and five nearby communities in the west and northwest San Fernando Valley. Dispatch is open 24/7, and each town below has its own page with local notes.

This page lists the ZIP codes we serve, explains how coverage works and describes how local geography changes the way water problems show up in a home.

Chatsworth, ZIP 91311

Chatsworth is a neighborhood of the City of Los Angeles in the west San Fernando Valley, at an elevation of 978 feet. Its standard ZIP is 91311, and 91313 is used for PO boxes only. Our office is at 20953 Devonshire Street.

The Santa Susana Mountains lie to the north and the Simi Hills to the west and north. If your home is near the foothills, tell dispatch, because slope and drainage can affect where water collects.

Northridge, ZIPs 91324, 91325 and 91330

Northridge sits east of Chatsworth, with Porter Ranch and Granada Hills to the north and Winnetka and Chatsworth to the west. Much of its housing grew from postwar tract building, and the 1994 earthquake lifted some structures off their foundations.

Older tract homes can hide long-running plumbing and slab moisture, so mention your home's age on the call. Our Northridge page has the local details.

Porter Ranch, ZIP 91326

Porter Ranch occupies the hilly northwest tip of the Valley at about 1,280 feet, with the Santa Susana Mountains to the north. Housing began around 1960 to 1963, and newer tracts followed after the area was annexed by Los Angeles in 1965.

Hillside lots send runoff downhill, so water entering a garage or lower level may come from above. Read about it on our Porter Ranch page, and tell dispatch if your home sits on a slope.

Canoga Park, ZIPs 91303 and 91304

Canoga Park borders Chatsworth to the north, Winnetka to the east, West Hills to the west and Woodland Hills to the south. It was annexed by Los Angeles in 1917 and has a mix of older homes and multi-unit buildings along its main streets.

In older buildings, a leak in one unit can travel through shared walls and floors. Our Canoga Park page covers more, and you should tell dispatch if neighbors are affected.

West Hills, ZIP 91307

West Hills formed as a neighborhood in 1987 from western Canoga Park, and part of ZIP 91304 also reaches it. It covers 8.5 square miles at about 899 feet, with the Chatsworth Reservoir area to the north and Bell Canyon in Ventura County to the west.

Homes here range from 1960s estates to newer builds, so pipe materials vary. Our West Hills page has the local notes, and your ZIP helps dispatch confirm you are in range.

Winnetka, ZIP 91306

Winnetka started in 1922 as a poultry colony and now sits between Chatsworth to the north and Woodland Hills to the south. The 2010 Census counted renters in 42% of its housing.

For renters, report a leak to the landlord or property manager at once, and call us if the landlord authorizes work. Details are on our Winnetka page.

Coverage is decided by your address and what dispatch can reach. Give your street address and ZIP when you call, and dispatch will tell you if a crew can reach you and what the next step is. Same-day response is offered when available.

We sometimes get calls from near Twin Lakes, an unincorporated Los Angeles County community north of Chatsworth, and from the Chatsworth Reservoir area. We do not have dedicated pages for them.

We do not claim coverage there from this website. Ask when you call, and dispatch will tell you whether a crew can reach your address.

How geography changes water problems

Chatsworth has a Mediterranean climate, with rain falling mostly from November through March and dry summers. Water problems cluster in the wet months, when roof leaks and drainage issues show up, but pipe failures and appliance leaks happen all year.

On hillsides, runoff moves downhill and can reach garages and lower levels. On the valley floor, water tends to spread across flat slabs and into wall bottoms. Older tracts may have aging supply lines, while newer tracts have different materials and layouts.

None of this changes the first step. Stop the source, keep safe from electrical risk and call. For flood concerns, check the FEMA flood map for your address, and remember that flood damage usually needs a separate flood policy.

Frequently asked questions

Which ZIP codes do you serve?

We focus on Chatsworth 91311, Northridge 91324, 91325 and 91330, Porter Ranch 91326, Canoga Park 91303 and 91304, West Hills 91307 and Winnetka 91306. Give dispatch your address and ZIP when you call to confirm a crew can reach you.

Do you serve Twin Lakes?

We sometimes hear from callers near Twin Lakes, but we have no dedicated page there and make no coverage claim on this site. Call and give your address. Dispatch will tell you whether a crew can reach you.

Is 91313 part of your area?

ZIP 91313 is used for PO boxes in Chatsworth, so it is not a street address area. If you have a PO box, give dispatch the street address of the property where the water damage happened, along with the 91311 or other ZIP.

Does the work differ by town?

The work does not differ much. Extraction, drying and mold remediation follow the same standards everywhere. What differs is the home: hillside lots, older tract homes and multi-unit buildings each have their own water patterns, and dispatch asks about them on the call.

What if my town is not listed?

Call anyway. We list the towns where we take most calls, but dispatch can tell you whether a crew can reach your address. Please do not wait while water spreads, because the sooner you call the more options you have.

Insurance notes for a Porter Ranch claim

Many policies respond to sudden, accidental water, and many do not respond to damage that built up slowly. Storm flooding usually sits outside a standard homeowners policy and needs flood coverage of its own. Read your policy and ask your insurer how it classifies storm water.

The California Department of Insurance tells policyholders to make temporary repairs that stop further damage and to keep the receipts. Take photos of everything, and hold off on large permanent repairs until the adjuster has looked at the loss.

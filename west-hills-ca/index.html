Pipes, heaters and who owns the leak

Platt Ranch, George Platt's former dairy, joined the Los Angeles water system in 1958. The utility there is LADWP. If the leak is at the meter or the street, ask LADWP who is responsible, and close the house valve in the meantime.

Inside the house, the usual causes are water heaters, supply lines under sinks and washing machine hoses. Water mitigation dries what the leak reached, using moisture meters to confirm every wall is back to normal.

Homes from the late 1950s and the 1960s

Platt Ranch stayed undeveloped until 1961, and the Corporate Pointe site was developed in 1959. Peppergate Ranch was subdivided into Woodlake and Pinelake Estates in the 1960s. Homes from those years may have original piping, so tell the dispatcher your house's age.

West Hills became a neighborhood in 1987, formed from western Canoga Park. Because the area was part of Canoga Park until then, give the dispatcher your cross streets if you are unsure which community you are in.

Slow leaks and mold

Mold needs moisture. A weeping fitting inside a wall can feed it for months. Mold work should follow ANSI/IICRC S520, and mold remediation begins by finding and fixing the moisture source.

Insurance notes for a West Hills claim

Coverage often follows how fast the water arrived: sudden and accidental events fare better than slow seepage. Flooding is typically excluded unless you bought flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

The California Department of Insurance advises telling your insurer at once, making temporary repairs and keeping receipts. For structural damage, ask licensed contractors for written bids that list every line.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency water removal available in West Hills?

Yes. Calls after midnight, on Sundays or on holidays reach the same dispatch line, and same-day response applies when a crew is available. Extraction units, air movers and moisture meters arrive with the crew. Close the source. If gas or wiring is involved, 911 or the utility comes before the restoration line.

Do you cover every ZIP code in West Hills?

Yes, ZIP code 91307 is covered, from the streets near the Chatsworth Reservoir area on the north, across Canoga Park on the east, to Woodland Hills and Hidden Hills on the south and southwest. Part of 91304 also reaches West Hills, so if your mail says 91304, the crews that serve Canoga Park also serve you.

Does canyon or hillside runoff count as flood damage?

Insurers usually treat water that comes in from outside as flood, and homeowners policies typically exclude it. Rules differ, so ask your insurer how your loss is classified. For cleanup, the crew treats outside water as contaminated and dries accordingly.

What if the leak is on the street side of the meter?

Close your house valve and call LADWP, since the utility decides who owns that part of the line, and ask what it needs from you. If water has already entered the house, call the restoration line too. Both calls can be made while you wait.

Can you tell me if mold is already in my wall?

Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras show which walls are wet, and any mold testing is expected to follow ANSI/IICRC S520. A restoration crew does not diagnose health issues, and it will not promise a result it cannot control. Ask your physician about symptoms and your adjuster about slow-leak coverage.

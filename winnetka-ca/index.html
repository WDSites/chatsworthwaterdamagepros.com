Water spreading in a Winnetka unit or house

Stop the source if you safely can. Close the valve under the fixture or the main valve, and tell the owner or manager at once. Finish with a call to dispatch, which sends a crew the same day when one is available.

On site, crews run extraction units first and read moisture meters to see what is wet. Water damage repair follows the drying. Drywall, flooring and paint come last, and in rentals the owner usually decides on them.

Do not wade through water near outlets or appliances. Switch power off only from a dry panel. If you suspect gas or live wiring, call 911 or the utility before ringing our line. Sewage calls for the same caution, which means staying out of it and keeping children and animals clear.

A renter's first steps

These actions protect your belongings and your position with the owner. Do them in this order.

  1. Close the water valve, then notify the landlord or manager in writing before the day ends.
  2. Photograph the source, the damage and your belongings, and list items with brand and model.
  3. Move valuables to dry ground and keep receipts for anything you buy to protect your things.
  4. With renters coverage, ask your insurer what it does for your belongings.

A landlord's first steps

For owners and managers, speed and records limit the damage. They also limit the disputes.

  1. Confirm the source is stopped and who was told.
  2. Notify neighboring units that water may be passing through walls, floors or ceilings.
  3. Arrange access and call for drying before scheduling any repair.
  4. Keep a log of calls, bids and messages, and check your policy before major work.

Insurance and responsibility in Winnetka

A pipe that bursts today is treated differently from one that has dripped for a year, and policies usually say so. Flood damage is normally a separate policy. Check your policy, read your lease and ask your insurer who handles which loss.

The California Department of Insurance advises temporary repairs to prevent further damage, plus receipts and a log of calls. If a claim is denied, the denial must come in writing with specific reasons and note that the Department can review it.

Yes. The dispatch line does not close for weekends or holidays, and same-day response is offered when available. The truck carries extraction units, air movers and moisture meters. Shut the source off, and leave any gas or wiring danger to 911 or the utility before you call us.

Who pays if I rent and a pipe breaks?

That depends on the lease, the cause and the policies involved. Tell the owner in writing, document everything and ask your insurer about renters coverage. A restoration crew cannot decide fault, but it can document the damage and the drying so everyone works from the same record.

Can I call for drying myself if the landlord is slow?

You can call, but the crew needs permission and access from the owner or manager for work on the building. Contents and your belongings are usually yours to protect. Keep written records of every request, and tell the crew who the contact is so access is not held up.

Should a landlord dry the unit before repairs?

Yes. Drying comes first, because covering damp walls with new drywall or paint traps moisture. Moisture meters confirm readings before any rebuild, and a written drying record helps with the insurer too. Rebuilding on damp framing is how mold starts.
